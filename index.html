About

Two homes,
one city

Heko Collection brings together two distinct apartments in the same corner of Athens — a design studio and a spacious two-bedroom flat, both at Rizari 15 in Pangrati. Whether you are travelling as a couple, solo, or with family, there is a Heko home for you.

Both apartments are within walking distance of the Panathenaic Stadium, the Cycladic Art Museum, Syntagma Square, and the Acropolis. Metro Evangelismos is 10 minutes on foot — or just 300 metres from the door.
