Heko Collection brings together two distinct apartments in the same corner of Athens — a design studio and a spacious two-bedroom flat, both at Rizari 15 in Pangrati.

About

Modern living in the centre
of ancient Athens

HeKo Modern is a two-bedroom apartment in the very heart of Athens — 600 metres from the Cycladic Art Museum and a comfortable walk from the Acropolis, the Temple of Olympian Zeus, and Monastiraki Square.

The apartment features an open-plan kitchen and dining area with warm parquet floors throughout, a fully equipped kitchen with a full-size refrigerator, and a private bathroom with washing machine. The arched interior layout gives the space a character rarely found in city-centre accommodation.
